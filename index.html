      Data notes
      
    
        
    Ocean-coast distances use Natural Earth’s ocean coastline. Inland seas such as the Caspian are not counted, so cities such as Baku appear far from the coast.
    
        
    Some elevations fall back to modeled terrain data and can be imprecise in steep areas. Cities below approximately −90 m are excluded from elevation rounds.

    The quick version
    
      
    How to play
    
      
    Make one numerical estimate in each of five rounds. An exact answer earns 1,000 points; scores fall smoothly as the estimate moves farther from the answer.
    
      
    
        
    City to cityEstimate the straight-line distance.
    
        
    Nearest coastEstimate the distance to the nearest ocean coastline.
    
        
    ElevationEstimate the city’s height above sea level.
    
      
    
      
    Metric and Imperial use identical scoring. Daily editions reset at 00:00 UTC and can be played once in this browser; random games are unlimited.
